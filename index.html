resumeBot: ask my AI assistant about me

resumeBot is an AI assistant I built as a learning project in retrieval-augmented generation (RAG): you ask a question about my experience, skills, education or writing, and it answers from my approved materials only. It first finds the sections of my background that best match your question, then a language model writes a short answer from those sections. It is not a general chatbot and it doesn't guess: when something isn't documented, such as salary expectations or availability, it says so and suggests asking me directly. It is also not a substitute for a conversation, so feel free to contact me!

By "AI answer," I mean an answer written by a large language model, the same kind of technology as ChatGPT, here mainly the open model gpt-oss-120b. The model runs on a free service with a daily usage limit that all visitors share. That's why each visitor gets three AI answers per day: it keeps the assistant available for everyone and the project free to run. Questions someone has already asked, and questions it can't answer, don't count. After your three AI answers, no language model writes anything. Instead, a much smaller model compares the meaning of your question with my materials, and you see the best-matching passages word for word. You get three new AI answers the next day.
